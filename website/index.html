Everything a modern classroom needs

Core monitoring plus school-specific tools — all in one place.

Live monitoring

Watch every student screen in real time — thumbnail wall or full-screen view, over wired or wireless networks.

Remote control

Take control of any machine instantly for demonstrations, troubleshooting or guided help.

Student lockdown

Block Command Prompt, PowerShell and other terminals. Students can't quit the client or reconfigure it — admins get instant alerts with timestamps.

Web filtering

Keep students on-task with built-in web filtering and site policies you control.

Audio classroom

Teacher voice broadcast and two-way audio for labs without headsets.

Timetable & sessions

Schedule lab sessions, auto-lock/unlock machines, and track usage over time.

Messaging

Send announcements, questions and instructions to the whole class or individual students.

Multi-display

Offline-licensed

Audit & reports
